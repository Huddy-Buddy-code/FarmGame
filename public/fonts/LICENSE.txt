The .pbf files in this directory are pre-rendered SDF glyph ranges of Noto Sans,
vendored so the game's map labels work with no live third-party font request at
play time. They were downloaded by `tools/fetch-glyphs.mjs` from
https://demotiles.maplibre.org/font (MapLibre's public demo glyph server).

The SIL OFL permits redistribution of the font (and derived/rendered forms such
as these glyph ranges) provided this notice is retained.
